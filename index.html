About Me

I am a dedicated Science Teacher with 13 years of teaching experience across different educational settings. My teaching journey has strengthened my commitment to creating engaging, inclusive, and meaningful learning experiences.

I believe science education should encourage students to ask questions, investigate ideas, think critically, and connect scientific concepts to real-life situations.

My instructional approach emphasizes inquiry-based learning, differentiated instruction, hands-on activities, laboratory safety, and the effective use of educational technology.
